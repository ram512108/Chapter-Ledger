# Chapterly Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 3, 2026**

Chapterly is an offline-first reading tracker. This policy describes what information the app handles.

## Information stored on your device

Chapterly stores your library and reading information locally on your Android device, including book titles, authors, ISBNs, chapters, reading progress, reading dates, notes, quotes, ratings, shelves, goals, and reading sessions. This information is not uploaded to a Chapterly server because the app does not operate a Chapterly account or cloud database.

## Book search and ISBN lookup

When you explicitly search for a book or look up an ISBN, the app sends the search term or ISBN to **Open Library** to retrieve book metadata. Cover images may also be loaded from Open Library. Please review Open Library's own policies for its handling of requests and content.

The app does not send your local reading notes, quotes, reading history, goals, or library to Open Library as part of normal book lookup.

## Export and sharing

The app provides JSON export and Goodreads CSV import features. Exported files or text can contain information from your local library. If you choose to copy, share, upload, or otherwise transfer an export, that transfer is controlled by you and the receiving service.

## Analytics and advertising

The production app does not intentionally include advertising, analytics, Firebase, Gemini, or account/authentication services.

## Permissions

The app requests internet access for Open Library book lookup, network-state access, and vibration for user-interface feedback. It does not request location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, or broad file-access permissions.

## Children's privacy

Chapterly is a general reading-tracker app and is not designed specifically for children.

## Security

Network communication with Open Library uses HTTPS. Local data is stored using Android application storage and the app's local database. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
