Privacy Policy for Chapterly

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chapterly ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

Chapterly is a book and reading-tracking application developed by Ramkumar R. The App helps users manage books, chapters, reading progress, notes, quotes, ratings, reading sessions, shelves, and reading goals.

Privacy Contact: ram512108@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Store in the App

Chapterly allows you to enter and store information such as:

  • Book titles
  • Authors
  • ISBNs
  • Reading status
  • Chapter information
  • Reading progress
  • Reading dates and sessions
  • Personal notes
  • Quotes
  • Ratings
  • Shelves
  • Reading goals
  • Other information that you voluntarily enter into the App

In the current version, this information is primarily stored locally on your device.

Chapterly does not currently require you to create an account.

1.2 Book Search and Metadata

The App provides an optional book-search and ISBN-lookup feature using the Open Library service.

When you search for a book or look up a book using an ISBN, the App may send the relevant search term or ISBN to Open Library to retrieve publicly available book information, such as:

  • Book title
  • Author information
  • ISBN information
  • Publication information
  • Book identifiers
  • Book cover information

The App may also retrieve book-cover images from Open Library.

Open Library is an external service operated by the Internet Archive. Its own policies apply to information processed through its services.

Open Library API:
https://openlibrary.org/developers/api

Open Library Covers API:
https://openlibrary.org/dev/docs/api/covers

1.3 Barcode Scanning

Chapterly provides an optional ISBN/barcode scanning feature.

When you choose to scan a barcode, the App uses Google's barcode-scanning functionality to identify the barcode.

The resulting barcode or ISBN may then be used to look up book information through Open Library if you choose to perform the lookup.

The App does not use barcode scanning to create an advertising profile or user account.

2. Information We Do Not Currently Collect

The current version of Chapterly does not intentionally collect or maintain personal information such as:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Contacts
  • Precise location
  • SMS or call information
  • Microphone recordings
  • Payment information
  • Advertising profiles

The current version does not require user registration or login.

The current version does not contain advertising functionality.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored in Chapterly is used to provide features such as:

  • Managing your personal book library
  • Tracking reading progress
  • Tracking chapter completion
  • Recording reading sessions
  • Creating notes and quotes
  • Managing shelves
  • Tracking reading goals
  • Displaying reading statistics
  • Looking up books through Open Library
  • Displaying book metadata and covers

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use your reading history or notes for targeted advertising in the current version.

4. Information Sharing

Chapterly does not currently operate a developer-owned server that stores your personal reading library.

Information you enter into the App is primarily stored locally on your device.

When you voluntarily use book-search or ISBN-lookup functionality, information necessary to perform that lookup may be sent to Open Library.

For example, if you search for a book title, the search request may be sent to Open Library so that the App can retrieve matching book information.

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your reading information is primarily stored locally on your device in the current version of the App.

We take reasonable measures in the design of the App to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of personal information.

However, no electronic storage system or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your device, including device access controls and operating-system security updates.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your reading information is primarily stored locally, you can manage or delete your information using the data-management features provided by the App.

If you use the App's export functionality, exported files are under your control and may remain wherever you choose to store or share them.

The current version of Chapterly does not maintain a separate developer-owned online copy of your personal reading library.
